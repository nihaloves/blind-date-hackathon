MOONLOOP: STRANDED is an interactive lunar survival simulation. You play the mission commander of a crew stuck on the Moon, waiting for a rescue that may or may not come on time. Your job is to keep everyone alive with whatever supplies you have left.



Along the way, you’ll juggle oxygen, water, food, power, habitat pressure, and crew health while things keep going wrong: equipment breaks, oxygen leaks, solar arrays get damaged. Problems rarely stay in one place. A power shortage can threaten life support, and a leak can drain your oxygen and put your crew at risk, so one failure can quickly ripple through the whole habitat. A rule-based decision engine models these dependencies and cascading failures.



You can make repairs, ration resources, put life-support systems first, and rethink your plan when rescue gets delayed. If you’re unsure what to do, a built-in mission intelligence module looks at the current state of the habitat and suggests a course of action, explaining the reasoning behind it so you can weigh the advice yourself.



Every choice has consequences. The simulation tracks them and judges the mission by whether the crew survives and how well the habitat holds up.



Beyond being a game, MOONLOOP: STRANDED works as a software prototype for exploring autonomous survival planning, risk assessment, and resilience in future lunar missions, combining resource management, scenario-based decisions, and interactive visualization.
